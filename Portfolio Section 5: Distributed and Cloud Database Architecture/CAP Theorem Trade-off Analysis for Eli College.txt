                  ystem Partition Trade-off Decisions
Consistency Over Availability (CP Choice for Grades & Registration)

Module: Grade Entry, Transcript Generation, and Seat Allocation.

Trade-off Justification: It is unacceptable for a student to view outdated or inaccurate grades due to replication lag. The system enforces Strong Consistency (CP), opting to briefly reject a write request if network partitioning prevents database nodes from agreeing on official records.

Availability Over Consistency (AP Choice for Student Dashboard & Catalog)

Module: Course Catalog Browsing, General Announcements, and Syllabus Lookup.

Trade-off Justification: Enforces Eventual Consistency (AP). Read queries draw from distributed read replicas. In the rare event of inter-node partition delay, serving slightly stale catalog information is preferred over denying access to the student portal entirely.
