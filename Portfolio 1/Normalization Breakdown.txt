To ensure data integrity and eliminate insert, update, and delete anomalies, the Eli College database schema was normalized from an unnormalized record format up to Third Normal Form (3NF). Unnormalized Form (UNF) A flat spreadsheet structure containing repeating groups:Unnormalized_Transcript (MatricNo, StudentName, DeptCode, DeptName, CourseCode, CourseTitle, CreditUnits, LecturerStaffNo, LecturerName, Semester, AcademicYear, Score, LetterGrade, GradePoint) First Normal Form (1NF) Rule: Eliminate repeating groups; ensure all attributes contain atomic values; define a composite Primary Key. Composite PK: (MatricNo, CourseCode, Semester, AcademicYear) Relation: UNF_1NF (MatricNo, StudentName, DeptCode, DeptName, CourseCode, CourseTitle, CreditUnits, LecturerStaffNo, LecturerName, Semester, AcademicYear, Score, LetterGrade, GradePoint) Second Normal Form (2NF) Rule: Must be in 1NF; eliminate partial functional dependencies (non-key attributes depending on only a portion of the composite key). Functional Dependencies (FDs): : MatricNo StudentName, DeptCode, DeptName : CourseCode CourseTitle, CreditUnits : LecturerStaffNo LecturerName : (CourseCode, Semester, AcademicYear) LecturerStaffNo : (MatricNo, CourseCode, Semester, AcademicYear) Score, LetterGrade, GradePoint Decomposed Tables (2NF): Students_2NF (MatricNo, StudentName, DeptCode, DeptName) Courses_2NF (CourseCode, CourseTitle, CreditUnits) Lecturers_2NF (LecturerStaffNo, LecturerName) Offerings_2NF (OfferingID, CourseCode, Semester, AcademicYear, LecturerStaffNo) Registrations_Grades_2NF (MatricNo, OfferingID, Score, LetterGrade, GradePoint) Third Normal Form (3NF) Rule: Must be in 2NF; eliminate transitive functional dependencies (non-key attributes depending on other non-key attributes).
