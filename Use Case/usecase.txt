Case Study Description: Eli College (EC) Student Result Portal
Eli College (EC) requires a centralized database management system to handle academic operations, course enrollments, grade entries, GPA computations, and student transcript generation.

The enterprise domain encompasses the following functional requirements:

Academic Structure: The college consists of multiple academic Departments (e.g., Computer Science, Electrical Engineering). Each department offers specific Courses and employs Lecturers.

Instructors & Students: Lecturers are assigned to lead specific course offerings each semester. Students belong to a primary department and enroll in courses offered across semesters.

Enrollment & Assessment: During registration periods, students create Registration Records for course offerings. Upon semester completion, lecturers submit numerical scores which translate into letter Grades and grade points.

Academic Evaluation: The portal computes Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) dynamically and produces official academic Transcripts.
